Local visibility in Abuja + service-intent rankings; feed enquiries; build topical authority in Abuja events.

## 3. Site architecture for SEO
One page per service; area hub + district pages (unique content only); journal pillars linking to service pages; pricing page targets cost queries; FAQ blocks with schema; breadcrumbs; flat, descriptive URLs.

## 4. Content pillars & seed articles (pre-launch)
1. How much does an event planner cost in Abuja? (2026 guide) — links to `/pricing`
2. Wedding budget in Abuja: where the money goes (traditional + white wedding)
3. Best event centres in Abuja by district (Maitama, Wuse 2, Jabi, Gwarinpa): capacity, price bands, what to check (power, parking, overtime)
4. Wedding planner vs day-of coordinator: which do you need?
5. Corporate event planning checklist for Abuja teams
6. How to plan an event from abroad (diaspora guide)
More: event timelines by type, vendor-vetting checklist, permits/security for events in FCT {{verify}}, seasonal calendar (Harmattan, rainy season, public holidays), real-event case studies.

Rules: original photos/data; author byline; update dates; cite sources; no AI-generic filler; each article ends with a relevant CTA.

## 5. Local SEO
- **Google Business Profile:** claim/verify; primary category *Event planner* (+ *Wedding planner*, *Event management company* if apt); service area Abuja; services list; photos weekly; Q&A seeded; posts 1–2/week; review-request script (WhatsApp) after each event; respond to all reviews.
- **NAP** identical everywhere (`site.ts`). Add WhatsApp Business profile with catalogue.
- **Citations:** Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook Page, Instagram bio (link + phone), niche directories (Wezoree, JoyRibbons, Babymigo, ElitePlanners.ng, Connect Nigeria, Finelib, Nigeria Yellow Pages — only if listing is free/quality).
- Embed Google Map link on Contact (facade — no heavy iframe on load).

## 6. Technical SEO
Static HTML, semantic markup, clean URLs, sitemap, robots, canonical, hreflang not needed, structured data (`seo.md`), image optimisation, CWV budgets, internal search-console monitoring, log 404s, 301 map, no thin/duplicate pages, `noindex` for thank-you/dev.

## 7. Link building (ethical)
Vendor/venue partner links (photographers, caterers, venues); PR (local blogs, Abuja Kulture features, wedding blogs like Nigerian Wedding); guest posts on vendor sites; sponsor/association listings (EPA Abuja if member); testimonials given to vendors; avoid paid link schemes.

## 8. Social ↔ web
Instagram bio → `/` (or `/portfolio`); UTM-tag links; repurpose journal articles as carousels; embed a curated static grid (not live embed).

## 9. Measurement & cadence
Weekly: Search Console queries/pages, GBP insights. Monthly: rankings for 20 keywords, organic sessions, enquiries by source, CWV. Quarterly: content refresh + gap analysis. Publish ≥ 2 articles/month for 6 months.

## 10. Risks
Directories outranking the site for head terms → target long-tail + local pack; duplicate area pages → require unique data; thin content → editorial bar; review manipulation → never buy reviews.
