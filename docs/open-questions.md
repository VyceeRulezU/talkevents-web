# Open Questions (do not guess — ask the client)

## Business & brand
1. Legal name, RC number, year founded, registered address? (trust + footer + schema)
2. Who are the principals/team, and will they appear on the site?
3. Which services are actually offered? (planning, coordination, day-of, decor/styling, production, corporate, memorials…)
4. Wedding vs corporate vs social — what is the revenue split and which do they want more of?
5. Typical guest counts and budgets; will pricing/ranges be public?
6. Service area: Abuja only, or nationwide/destination?
7. Associations, awards, notable clients (with permission to display)?
8. Is "Talk Events" the trading name for the domain, and is there a preferred domain name/TLD?

## Contact & operations
9. Phone/WhatsApp number(s), email, office address or "by appointment"? Opening hours?
10. Response-time promise (e.g. within 24h)? Consultation process (free call? paid?)?
11. Booking process: deposit, contract, cancellation — state on site?
12. Who receives leads and how fast can they respond? (drives WhatsApp/email setup)

## Brand & design
13. **SVG/vector masters** of the logo and brand guideline (the JPEGs have backgrounds).
14. Confirm fonts: is the sample's serif/sans/script the desired look, or should we choose a more refined serif? (Sample fonts matched by eye.)
15. Any colours besides blue `#1A3870` and green `#7ED958` (e.g. a warm neutral/gold)?
16. Photo library: how many quality photos exist, who owns rights, are guests' consents recorded?
17. Video available? (reels/highlights) — hero video or images only?

## Digital
18. Domain name, TLD and registrar login access; existing email on the domain (MX records)?
19. Existing Google Business Profile? Existing reviews? Instagram follower count and top content?
20. Any ads planned (Meta/Google)? Newsletter desired?
21. Who will update content after launch (client vs builder)? CMS needed in v1?
22. Legal review of Privacy/Terms — client's counsel?

## Future app
23. What would a client portal need to do first (proposal approval, checklist, payments, guest list)? Any timeline or budget?
