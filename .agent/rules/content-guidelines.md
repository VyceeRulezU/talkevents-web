# Content Guidelines

- **Never fabricate**: years in business, event counts, clients, awards, testimonials, prices, RC number. Use `{{PLACEHOLDER: what is needed}}` and log it in `docs/open-questions.md`.
- **Voice:** warm, organised, reassuring; the brand promise is *reliability of execution* ("Your plan, perfectly executed"). Show process and proof over adjectives.
- **Nigerian English**; sentence case headings; short paragraphs; scannable subheads; ₦ with thousands separators; +234 phone formatting; use area names people search (Maitama, Wuse 2, Jabi, Gwarinpa, Asokoro, Garki, Katampe, Life Camp, Kubwa).
- **Cultural fluency:** weddings often span introduction/traditional + white wedding; use terms couples use ("traditional wedding", "engagement", "aso ebi coordination", "owambe" only if brand-appropriate). Respect religious and ethnic diversity in imagery and copy.
- **Proof:** testimonials with name + event type + written consent; case studies (brief → plan → result → photos); vendor partner mentions only with permission.
- **CTAs:** one primary CTA per section; consistent labels ("Plan my event", "Chat on WhatsApp", "Request a proposal").
- **Length targets:** service page 500–800 words; area page 400–600 unique words; journal posts 900–1,800 words with original photos/data.
- **Legal:** claims must be true and substantiable; obtain image and testimonial consent; avoid naming competitors.
- **Editorial process:** brief → draft → client fact-check → SEO check (`seo-audit`) → publish → update `lastmod`.
